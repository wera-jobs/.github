# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the [wera-jobs](https://github.com/wera-jobs) organization and the service at wera-jobs.com.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to **jessechumo@gmail.com**, or use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability) on the repository if it is turned on. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the impact you expect.

## What to expect

- We reply within 3 business days.
- We confirm or rule out the issue and tell you what we plan to do.
- We fix serious issues first and ship them as patch releases.
- We credit you when the fix ships, if you would like that.

## Good-faith research

We will not pursue action against research that:

- stays within your own account and test data;
- avoids reading, changing or deleting other people's data;
- avoids service disruption, including denial of service and heavy automated scanning;
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before it is made public.

If you reach someone else's data by accident, stop, do not keep a copy, and tell us.

## Out of scope

- Social engineering of staff or users
- Physical attacks
- Reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact
- Missing best-practice headers with no security effect

## Supported versions

Only the latest release of each repository receives security fixes.
