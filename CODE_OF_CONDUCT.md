# Code of conduct

Wera exists to help people through a stressful part of their lives. We expect the same care from everyone who works with us, in issues, pull requests, and the Wera community.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers. Not everyone shares your background, country or first language.
- Accept correction gracefully, and correct your own mistakes.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning language about anyone's identity, background, nationality or immigration status
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Sexual content or unwanted advances
- Spam, or any other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **jessechumo@gmail.com**. Reports are handled in confidence.

## What happens next

We will review each report and may respond with a warning, remove content, or ban someone from our spaces, either for a period or permanently. We apply this to everyone, including maintainers.

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
